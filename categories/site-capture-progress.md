---
name: Site capture and progress tracking
layer: build
order: 1
question: Does the firm know, objectively and weekly, what has actually been built?
---
Cameras (often 360° cameras on a helmet), drones and laser scanners capture the
site; computer vision (AI that interprets images) maps the images to the plans
and compares what's built against the model and the programme.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** it turns the site into data. Progress,
quality and payment discussions can rest on evidence instead of opinion, and
the captured history becomes training data for better planning. It is a
relatively mature category with many large-contractor deployments.

**Watch for:** the value depends on teams acting on what the system shows, not
just capturing it.
