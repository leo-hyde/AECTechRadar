---
A "digital twin" is a model of a building or infrastructure asset that is kept
up to date with real data (sensors, inspections, maintenance records), so
owners can monitor, simulate and plan maintenance.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** it extends a contractor's role
beyond handover into long-term services, and closes the loop: data on how
assets actually perform can improve the next design. It is also where the
business-model change (from one-off projects to ongoing services) is most
visible.

**Watch for:** "digital twin" is used loosely in marketing; ask what data
stays live and who keeps it updated.
