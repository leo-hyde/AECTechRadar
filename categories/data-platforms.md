---
name: Project data platforms
layer: foundation
order: 1
question: Where does all project data live, and can AI reach it?
---
The systems of record for drawings, models, RFIs (requests for information),
submittals, costs and site logs. Most large firms already run one or more.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** AI can only reason over data it can
reach. A firm whose project history sits in clean, connected platforms can
train, search and automate across every project; one whose data sits in email
and shared drives can't. In 2025–26 the big platforms (Procore, Autodesk,
Bentley, Nemetschek) all added AI assistants and "agents" (AI that carries out
multi-step tasks) on top of the data they hold, so choosing a platform is now
also choosing an AI strategy.

**What to ask a client:** how many systems hold project data, who owns the
data contractually, and whether it can be exported in open formats.
