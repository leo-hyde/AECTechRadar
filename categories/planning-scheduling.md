---
name: Planning, scheduling and risk forecasting
layer: plan
order: 2
question: Can AI build better programmes and predict delays before they happen?
---
Two different approaches: **optimisation** (software that generates and
compares many possible construction sequences) and **forecasting** (machine
learning trained on thousands of past schedules to predict where a programme
will slip).

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** delay is the industry's biggest
money-loser. A firm that can test scenarios in hours and price schedule risk
from evidence rather than workshops can bid and manage differently. This is
one of the clearest cases of AI changing a core business decision rather than
just saving admin time.

**Watch for:** forecasting needs lots of historical schedules; ask whose data
the model learned from and whether the firm's own data improves it.
