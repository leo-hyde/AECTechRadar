name: Document and contract intelligence
layer: foundation
order: 2
question: Can AI read, check and answer questions about the project's documents?
---
AI that reads drawings, specifications, contracts, RFIs and submittals:
answering questions, flagging risky clauses, checking submittals against the
spec, and spotting changes between drawing revisions.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** construction runs on documents, and
most of the knowledge a site team needs is buried in them. This is where large
language models (the technology behind ChatGPT and Claude) fit most naturally,
so it is one of the fastest-moving categories. It is also consolidating fast:
platforms are building or buying these features (for example Trimble agreeing
to buy Document Crunch in 2026).

**Watch for:** answers without citations, and who is accountable when an AI
misses a clause.
