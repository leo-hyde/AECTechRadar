question: Can AI speed up quantity take-off, estimating and bidding?
---
"Take-off" is measuring quantities (areas, lengths, counts) from drawings to
price a job. AI tools now detect and measure rooms, walls and fittings on
drawings automatically, and help compare subcontractor bids.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** estimating is repetitive, deadline-
driven and decides which jobs a firm wins and whether they make money. Faster
take-off means more bids, or more time spent on judgement rather than
measurement.

**Watch for:** accuracy claims are almost always the vendor's own; ask for
trials on the firm's own past drawings.
