question: Can building move from the site to the factory, and is it worth it?
---
Modular, prefabricated and factory-built components, and the design-for-
manufacture approach (designing so parts can be made in a factory) behind
them.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** factories generate consistent data
and repeatable processes, which suit automation far better than one-off sites.
But this category has a hard history: several heavily funded firms failed,
most visibly Katerra in 2021. Any transformation plan that leans on offsite
needs to show it has learned those lessons.
