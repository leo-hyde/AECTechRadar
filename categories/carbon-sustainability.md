question: Can the firm measure and cut the carbon in what it designs and builds?
---
Tools for life-cycle assessment (LCA: the environmental impact of a building
from materials to demolition), embodied carbon, environmental product
declarations (EPDs: standard statements of a product's impact) and regulatory
reporting.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** carbon reporting is increasingly a
regulatory and client requirement, and it depends on detailed material data,
which is exactly what AI can help gather and keep up to date. Firms that link
their design models to carbon data can compare options on carbon as easily as
on cost.
