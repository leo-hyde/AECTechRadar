---
name: Design and feasibility automation
layer: design
order: 1
question: Can AI generate and test design options faster than people can?
---
"Generative design" tools: software that produces many layout or massing
options from rules and goals (site, unit mix, parking, cost) so people can
compare them, plus AI assistants inside design software.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** it moves effort from drawing options
to choosing between them, and lets a contractor or developer test feasibility
before paying for full design. For design-and-build firms it can connect
design choices to cost and programme early.

**Watch for:** options that look good but ignore local planning rules, and
how outputs move into the firm's main design tools (Revit and others).
