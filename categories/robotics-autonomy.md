name: Construction robotics and autonomy
layer: build
order: 2
question: Which physical site tasks can machines now do reliably?
---
Robots and autonomy kits for specific, repetitive tasks: marking out layouts
on floors, finishing drywall, driving piles, trenching, drilling. Most are
offered as "robots as a service" (rented per job, with support) rather than
sold.

**Why it matters for an AI-native firm:** labour shortages and safety. Robots
are where AI reaches the physical work, but they succeed task by task, not
as general-purpose site workers, and they need the digital model to be right.

**Watch for:** speed claims are usually from the vendor; ask what the site has
to look like for the robot to work (clear floors, finished slabs, connectivity).
