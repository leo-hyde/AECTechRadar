**What it is:** an annual ranking (the sixth edition in 2025) of construction
tech startups, in four areas: Green Construction, Enhanced Productivity,
Construction Supply Chain and Future of Construction.

**Headline data quoted with it:** Contech investment in 2024 of about
$3.1 billion across 325 deals; deal count up about 38% but money up only about
2.3% on 2023; Enhanced Productivity 47% of deals, Green Construction 24%.

**Useful for:** finding startups to add to this radar, especially in Europe
and Latin America.

**Caveats:** the full list wasn't read in this pass. Cemex Ventures is an
investor, not a neutral judge.
