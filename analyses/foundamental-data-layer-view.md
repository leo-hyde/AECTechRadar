**Why it's here:** Foundamental is a specialist construction-tech venture
firm (its newer fund targeted $85m for early-stage startups). One of its
partners, Shub Bhattacharya, argues that lasting value in construction AI will
come from data infrastructure and systems of record rather than thin AI-only
apps (per his VC Sheet profile: https://www.vcsheet.com/who/shub-bhattacharya).

**Useful for:** the central strategic question for an AI-native firm: own the
data layer, or rent AI features from platforms?

**Caveats:** the argument is summarised from a profile page, not a published
report; find a primary piece by the author before quoting.
