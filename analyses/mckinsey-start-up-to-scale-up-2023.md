**What it says (from summaries; the full PDF wasn't read in this pass):**
AEC is a roughly $12 trillion industry that has been slow to digitise.
Investment in AEC tech reached $50 billion between 2020 and 2022. The authors
mapped more than 3,000 AEC tech companies and expect consolidation to
continue. Demand for infrastructure, labour shortages and pressure for data
transparency are driving adoption.

**Useful for:** the scale-of-market framing and the argument that integration
and consolidation will decide which tools survive.

**Caveats:** written before the 2024–26 wave of generative AI and agents.
Figures like "talent investment raises the odds of digital success 2.5×" come
from a secondary summary (BDC Network) and should be checked in the PDF.
