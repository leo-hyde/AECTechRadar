**What it says:** in Q2 2025, $3.96 billion went into built-environment tech
startups; of 159 AEC deals, 110 involved AI/ML companies, which took about
$2.71 billion (about 68%).

**Useful for:** evidence that investment is now concentrated in AI, which
matters when judging which categories will keep improving fastest.

**Caveats:** different trackers disagree. Cemex Ventures put AI's share of
2025 contech funding at 77%; Superscout put it near two-thirds. Definitions of
"AI company" and "contech" differ, so compare trends, not exact numbers.
