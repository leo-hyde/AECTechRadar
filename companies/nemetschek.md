## What it does
A group of design, engineering and construction software brands, including
Archicad and Allplan. Since 2024 it has been building one AI layer that all its products
share, starting with an AI assistant in Archicad and Allplan.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
For firms on Archicad or Allplan, this is the AI that will
arrive inside tools they already use; worth comparing with Autodesk's
approach.

## Evidence and caveats
- Only Archicad and Allplan are confirmed by the sources here; check the full
  brand list on Nemetschek's site. Headquarters and founding year not checked.
