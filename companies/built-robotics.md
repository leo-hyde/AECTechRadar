## What it does
Retrofits standard heavy equipment with sensors and software so it can work
autonomously. Its RPD 35 robot surveys, distributes and drives piles for solar
farms; its Exosystem kit enables autonomous trenching. Rented as a service.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
Shows the pattern for site autonomy: narrow, repetitive, well-defined tasks on
open sites first. It also partners with the International Union of Operating
Engineers on training, a model for handling workforce concerns.

## Evidence and caveats
- Speed claims (up to 5× faster) are the company's. Some company pages are
  several years old, so current focus should be confirmed.
