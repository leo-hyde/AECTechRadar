## What it does
Calculates the environmental impact of buildings and products across their
life, produces environmental product declarations (EPDs), and supports
regulatory and certification reporting, with large libraries of material data.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
Carbon is becoming a design constraint like cost. A firm that can compare
options on carbon automatically, from its models, has an advantage in bids
with carbon targets.

## Evidence and caveats
- Usage claims (150+ countries) are the company's own.
