## What it does
The robot maps the room with lidar and 3D sensing, then sprays and sands
joint compound; people tape and do corners and touch-ups. Canvas works as a
subcontractor, training and employing union finishers to run it.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
A good example of robotics changing a trade's business model (a tech company
acting as subcontractor), not just its tools.

## Evidence and caveats
- Founded 2017 inside Otherlab (trade press; total funding about $43m as of
  2021). No later funding news found.
- Time savings (5–7 days down to about 2) are the company's claims.
