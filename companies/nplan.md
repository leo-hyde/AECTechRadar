## What it does
Instead of risk workshops where experts guess how long activities might slip,
nPlan's models learn from a large archive of historical schedules (the company
cites more than 750,000) and give a probabilistic forecast for each activity,
rolled up into a project view of delay risk.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
It replaces expert opinion with evidence from past projects, which is the
heart of the "AI at the centre" idea: decisions grounded in the firm's (and
the industry's) data. It also shows the value of a firm keeping its own
schedule history.
