## What it does
Design software (including Revit) plus Forma, a cloud platform that now spans
early design (Forma Site Design, Forma Building Design), data management
(formerly Autodesk Docs) and construction (the former Autodesk Construction
Cloud). Autodesk Assistant and a growing set of AI agents sit across it.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
For many firms, Autodesk holds the design data that every other AI tool needs.
How open that data is (and on what terms) shapes what the firm can build
itself versus buy from Autodesk.
