## What happened
Katerra tried to own the whole chain: design, its own factories for building
components, and construction. It raised very large sums, made many
acquisitions and took on fixed-price contracts. It filed for bankruptcy in
2021 after cost overruns and losses; its own filing also blamed the pandemic
and losing access to construction bonding after its lender (Greensill)
collapsed.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
It is the obvious cautionary tale for any "transform the whole business"
strategy. Commentators broadly agree on overreach (too many initiatives at
once), weak execution and unprofitable project economics; they disagree on how
much the financing shocks were the cause versus the trigger.

## The lesson for advice
Transform in stages that each pay back; don't buy the whole supply chain
before the core process works; and keep the economics of each project visible.
