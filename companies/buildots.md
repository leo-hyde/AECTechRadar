## What it does
Site teams walk the site with a 360° camera on a helmet (or use drones or
scans). Computer vision works out what has been installed, element by element,
and compares it with the BIM model and the programme, producing progress
tracking and early warning of delays. The company says captures are processed
within 24–36 hours and that it now covers groundworks to fit-out.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
It turns "how far along are we?" from an opinion into data, every week, across
every project. That data feeds planning, payments and future forecasting.
