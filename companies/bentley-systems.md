## What it does
Engineering and project-delivery software for infrastructure (products
named in the sources include ProjectWise, SYNCHRO and OpenSite+), with iTwin as a platform for digital twins
that combine engineering models, reality data and sensors. AI features include
generative civil site design, 4D sequencing and an assistant.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
For an infrastructure contractor, the long-term opportunity is operating
assets, not just building them; digital twins are how that data stays live
after handover.
