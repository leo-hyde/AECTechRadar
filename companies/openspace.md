## What it does
A camera on a hard hat records a site walk; OpenSpace's AI places each image
on the floor plan automatically, building a time-stamped visual record of the
site that teams can browse like Street View. In October 2025 it bought
Disperse, a progress-tracking company (reported in search results; the
acquisition announcement itself wasn't read in this pass).

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
A complete visual history of every project is a foundation for later AI uses
(progress, quality, claims, training data), and capturing it costs little site
time.
