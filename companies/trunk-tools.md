## What it does
Builds a connected map of a project's documents and runs AI agents on top:
answering site questions from the documents, checking submittals against the
spec, spotting changes between drawing revisions, and helping with RFIs and
contracts. It integrates with Autodesk Forma.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
This is close to what "AI at the centre" looks like day to day: every project
document is searchable and checked automatically, and agents do first drafts
of routine work for people to approve.
