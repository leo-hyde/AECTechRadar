## What it does
Upload a set of drawings and the software finds and measures rooms, walls and
other elements automatically, producing quantities for estimators to check and
price.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
Estimating decides what a firm wins and whether it makes money. Faster take-off
lets the same team bid more work or spend time on judgement. It's also an easy
place to measure AI's value: compare against past jobs.

## Evidence and caveats
- Speed and accuracy claims vary by source (80% faster; 97% accuracy; up to
  90% less time) and all trace back to the company.
