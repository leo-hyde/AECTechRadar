## What it does
Instead of crews measuring and chalking lines, the robot reads the BIM model
and prints the layout (walls, openings, services) on the slab, to a stated
1/16-inch accuracy. Offered as a service priced by project size.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
It's a direct bridge from the digital model to the physical site: if the model
is right, the layout is right. That rewards firms whose models are accurate,
which is part of the AI-native story.

## Evidence and caveats
- Customer results (e.g. Skanska: 50% faster layout) are company case studies.
- No funding news after 2022 found; current funding not checked.
