## What it does
Cloud software for running construction projects: documents, drawings, RFIs,
submittals, budgets, daily logs and more. "Helix" is its AI layer: an
assistant, pre-built agents, and Agent Builder for firms to make their own.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
If a firm's project data already lives in Procore, its AI features can work
across that data without extra integration. The strategic question is whether
to build the firm's AI on a platform's agents (fast, but tied to that vendor)
or on its own data layer (more control, more work).
