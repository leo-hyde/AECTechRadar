## What it does
Users set a site, building type and targets (units, parking, cost), and the
software generates and scores many layout options, including multi-site
studies.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
Moves early design from days of drawing to minutes of comparing, so a
design-and-build contractor can test more opportunities and price them
sooner.
