## What it does
Reads construction contracts and flags risky or unusual clauses (payment,
indemnity, notice periods), then helps project teams find and follow the
obligations during the job. It has added conversational AI and a Microsoft
Word add-in.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
Contract risk is where small misses cost a lot. AI that reads every contract
the same way, every time, and puts obligations in front of site teams is a
clear case of AI reducing commercial risk.
