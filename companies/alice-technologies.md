## What it does
"Generative scheduling": a planner describes the work, crews and constraints,
and the software generates many feasible schedules and lets the team compare
them (cost, duration, resources). ALICE Pro starts from a BIM model; ALICE
Optimize works on existing Primavera P6 or Microsoft Project schedules. It
markets heavily to industrial work (data centres, energy, mining) as well as
commercial building.

## Why it matters for an AI-native firm
Scheduling is a core decision, not admin. Being able to test "what if we add a
second crane / change the sequence" in hours changes how a firm bids and
recovers delays. It is one of the clearer examples of AI informing a business
decision rather than saving clerical time.
